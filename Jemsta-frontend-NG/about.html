History and Background

Jemsta Real Estate & Management LTD is an international property investment and professional services company established to create opportunities for individuals and institutional investors seeking to build wealth through strategic real estate investments in the United Kingdom.

Jemsta Real Estate & Management is the Nigerian investment and property management arm of Jemsta Group of Companies. The Group brings together specialist companies with expertise across the entire property lifecycle — from sourcing investment opportunities and arranging finance to legal services, property management, maintenance, development, and sales. By integrating these services under one corporate umbrella, Jemsta Group offers investors a seamless, transparent, and professionally managed investment experience.

Jemsta Group of Companies

Five specialist companies, built up over time, each strengthening a different stage of the investment journey.

The foundations of the Group were laid with the establishment of Mortgage2Match Financial Services Ltd in September 2017. Created to provide expert mortgage and financial advice, the company has assisted numerous clients in securing residential and investment finance, laying the financial cornerstone upon which the wider Group would later be built.

Mortgage2Match

Jemsta Estate Agency

In May 2018, Jemsta UK Ltd, trading as Jemsta Estate Agency, was established to expand the Group's capabilities into estate agency, property sourcing, lettings, sales, and property management. The company was created to support investors by identifying quality investment opportunities, managing acquired properties, and maximising rental returns while protecting long-term asset values.

Recognising the importance of legal expertise within property transactions, ASK & Partners Legal Solutions Ltd was incorporated in August 2024. The company provides legal support across the Group, ensuring compliance, risk management, contractual integrity, dispute resolution, and legal guidance throughout every stage of property acquisition, development, and disposal.

ASK & Partners Legal Solutions

Anything Property Maintenance

As the Group's activities expanded into property improvement and development, Anything Property Maintenance Ltd was established in September 2025. The company specialises in property maintenance, refurbishment, renovation, and major development projects — enhancing the value of investment properties and delivering quality workmanship that supports sustainable long-term returns for investors.

To strengthen investment links between the United Kingdom and Nigeria, Jemsta Real Estate & Management Ltd was established in Nigeria in November 2025. The company was formed with a clear objective — to provide Nigerian investors with direct access to professionally managed UK property investments, while also facilitating carefully structured property development opportunities within Nigeria.

Through Jemsta Real Estate & Management Ltd, investors can participate in several investment models, including:

  • Purchasing investment properties in the United Kingdom.
  • Pooling investment funds to finance residential and commercial developments in Nigeria.
  • Participating in property development and flipping projects designed to generate attractive returns.
  • Investing in housing developments that make home ownership more accessible through structured mortgage solutions for Nigerian buyers.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What does Jemsta Real Estate & Management do?

We are the Nigerian investment and property management arm of Jemsta Group, giving investors in Nigeria direct access to professionally managed UK property investments, alongside carefully structured development opportunities within Nigeria.

Who can invest with Jemsta Real Estate & Management?

Any individual or institutional investor based in Nigeria who wants to build wealth through UK real estate — whether you're investing for the first time or growing an existing portfolio.

What investment options are available?

You can purchase investment properties in the UK, pool funds with other investors to finance residential and commercial developments in Nigeria, participate in property development and flipping projects, or invest in housing developments with structured mortgage solutions for Nigerian buyers.

How do I get started?

Get in touch to arrange an investment consultation. We'll assess your goals and budget, then guide you through financial assessment, mortgage arrangement, property sourcing, legal due diligence and purchase completion.

Do I need to travel to the UK to invest?

No. We manage the entire process on your behalf — from sourcing and legal checks to purchase and ongoing management — so you can invest from anywhere in Nigeria.

How is financing arranged?

Our sister company, Mortgage2Match Financial Services, provides expert mortgage and financial advice, helping investors secure competitive residential and investment finance.

Who handles the legal side of my investment?

ASK & Partners Legal Solutions, part of the Group, manages compliance, contracts and due diligence at every stage of your acquisition, development or disposal.

Who manages my property after purchase?

Jemsta Estate Agency handles lettings, sales and day-to-day property management, while Anything Property Maintenance takes care of refurbishment and upkeep — so your investment stays in excellent condition and continues earning rental income in pounds sterling.

Which areas do you operate in?

We work with investors across Nigeria and source, purchase and manage property investments across the United Kingdom.
