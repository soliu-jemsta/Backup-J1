Overview

One ecosystem, five specialisms.

Unlike traditional property companies that offer only one part of the journey, Jemsta Group brings together specialist businesses covering sourcing, finance, legal, development and management — so investors reach every service they need through a single trusted organisation.

Origin

The Group was established to bridge the gap between investors seeking secure, profitable property investments and the professional expertise required to identify, finance, acquire, develop, manage and dispose of those investments successfully.

Model

Five specialist companies work to one standard and a shared outcome — each providing expertise within a defined area of the property lifecycle, while collaborating to deliver a complete investment solution.

Reach

The Group continues to expand its footprint, connecting investors in Nigeria with carefully selected opportunities in the United Kingdom, while developing residential and commercial projects that contribute to economic growth in Nigeria.

Governance

Discipline across two jurisdictions.

Each specialist company is separately incorporated and regulated for its own activity. The Group maintains a shared standard of governance, professional due diligence and risk management across every business.

Structure

A defined structure separates specialist execution — mortgage advice, estate agency, legal services, construction, and investment coordination — from the shared standards the Group asks every company to meet.
