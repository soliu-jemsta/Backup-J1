Who We Are

Mortgage2Match Financial Services is an independent mortgage and protection practice founded by Stanley Adekola, bringing experience from both the banking sector and independent brokerage. We provide advice across mortgages, protection and general insurance, and we work with clients not just locally but across England, Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.

We're committed to putting clients at the heart of everything we do, and to building long-term relationships rather than one-off transactions. That starts with genuinely understanding your situation — your goals, your budget and the things that keep you up at night — before we ever start searching for a deal.
